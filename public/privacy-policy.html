Privacy Policy for Apex Rivals

This Privacy Policy describes how Apex Rivals ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in all our data handling practices in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play User Data policies.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect and process data necessary to provide competitive racing telemetry, multiplayer ghost competition, and personalized AI race coaching.

A. Telemetry and Racing Performance Data (Stored Locally)

  • Lap Times and Sector Times: Track performance metrics, lap splits, top speeds, and delta comparisons.
  • Physics Telemetry Snapshots: Throttle percentage, brake pressure, steering angle, G-forces, RPM, and speed values.
  • Local Storage: Stored securely on your device using a local SQLite Room database (race_results and race_sessions tables). This data remains on the device unless you explicitly choose to sync cloud data.

B. Driver Profile & Authentication Data (Optional)

  • Driver Handle: Your chosen in-game alias or driver display name.
  • Authentication: We use Google Firebase Authentication. Users may race as an Anonymous Guest or sign in using their Google account to preserve driver progress and leaderboard data.
  • Cloud Leaderboard Sync: Best lap times and driver ratings may be synced to Google Cloud Firestore to enable global competitive rankings.

C. Artificial Intelligence Interactions (Google Gemini API)

  • AI Race Coach & Engineer: Queries submitted in the AI Coach chat (such as tire degradation questions or setup adjustments) and prompt text for vehicle livery concepts may be sent to the Google Gemini API for analysis.
  • No Personal Identifiers in AI Prompts: We do not transmit personally identifiable information such as real names, email addresses, or phone numbers to AI models. Only functional racing context is used where necessary.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

Apex Rivals requests only the minimum permissions required for core app operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to connect to multiplayer lobbies, fetch leaderboard standings, sync optional cloud backups with Firebase, and query the Gemini AI Racing Coach.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to detect internet availability and switch smoothly between offline local physics mode and online esports multiplayer synchronization.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used for haptic feedback during gear shifts, curb vibrations, redline warnings, and tire lockup sensations.

Zero Sensitive Permissions: The App does NOT request access to your camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, phone state, or external storage files.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To simulate real-time vehicle dynamics and provide instant 60Hz telemetry analysis.
  • To calculate leaderboards, lap deltas, and cross-platform esports rankings.
  • To provide generative vehicle livery concepts and race engineer setup advice.
  • To save your race replay ghosts for personal skill development.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party providers that adhere to strict data security standards:

  • Google Play Services: Application distribution, in-app updates, and licensing. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase (Auth & Firestore): Secure authentication and cloud leaderboard synchronization. (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google Gemini API: Generative AI Race Engineer coaching and livery generation. (Google AI Terms)

5. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

You have full control over your data:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can clear all saved race sessions and lap records at any time by navigating to Garage & Social > Clear Local Data or by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Apex Rivals > Storage > Clear Data).
  • Cloud Data Deletion Request: If you signed in with Firebase and wish to delete your cloud profile and leaderboard entries permanently, email us at abux.one@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Apex Rivals". We will verify and erase your cloud records within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Apex Rivals is intended for general audiences and esports racing enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at abux.one@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete such information.

7. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and external servers (Firebase and Gemini API) is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Local telemetry databases are isolated in private Android sandbox storage and cannot be accessed by other applications.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Effective Date".
