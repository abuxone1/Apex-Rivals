Request data deletion

You can remove local race data by clearing Apex Rivals app storage in your Android settings. Cloud profile, authentication, leaderboard, and related data can be deleted by sending an email request.

Submit a request

  1. Email abux.one@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Data Deletion Request - Apex Rivals.
  3. Include the account email or driver handle associated with the data. Do not send passwords or private credentials.

We may ask for reasonable verification before completing a request. See the Privacy Policy for more information.
